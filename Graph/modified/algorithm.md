## Algorithm -

1.Start the program.

2.Read the number of vertices and edges.

3.Read the edges and create an adjacency list.

4.Accept the starting vertex from the user.

5.Mark all vertices as unvisited.

6.Create a queue and insert the starting vertex into it.

7.Mark the starting vertex as visited.

8.Repeat the following steps until the queue becomes empty:

•Remove a vertex from the queue and display it.

•Check all its neighbouring vertices.

•If a neighbour is unvisited, mark it as visited and insert it into the queue.

9.Display the BFS traversal order.

10.Display all visited vertices as reachable vertices.

11.Display all unvisited vertices as unreachable vertices.

12.Count the reachable vertices and display the total count, or display the BFS levels of the vertices.

13.Stop the program.
